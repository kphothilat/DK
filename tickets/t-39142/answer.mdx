## Draft reply

502 errors after a PR is most likely a proxy misconfiguration (Cloudflare, nginx, AWS CloudFront, etc.). Test your `*.mintlify.app` origin URL directly (e.g. `yoursite.mintlify.app`). If that loads fine, Mintlify's build and infra are healthy and the 502 is coming from your custom domain's proxy.

1. If the `.mintlify.app` URL also fails, then yes — retry the deployment manually from the dashboard using the re-trigger button on the failed deployment.
2. If the retry also fails with no clear error, that warrants a support escalation for engineering investigation.
