<Info>
Based on what I can see: both the TXT and CNAME records are typically required for validation to complete — the TXT record alone is usually not sufficient. The CNAME is what actually routes traffic and often what the validation check confirms is in place.

I'd recommend adding the CNAME record and then retrying validation. If that still fails, we can escalate further and have our team run diagnostics on your domain.
</Info>
