## Draft reply

**Issue 1 — 404 errors:**

A couple of different issues can be causing this:
- **Caching** — the page may be cached as a 404; a deployment or cache purge can fix it.
- **Slug/path mismatch** — the page path in your `mint.json` navigation may not match the file name.
- **Deployment not triggered** — the latest changes may not have been deployed yet.

Can you provide the domain and page path? Support will check the cache and routing state directly.

**Issue 2 — Custom domain:**

We'll connect you to the support team directly so they can run diagnostics on docs.paycode.com.mx, as it requires Mintlify support to investigate your domain's DNS health and Vercel/Cloudflare routing.
