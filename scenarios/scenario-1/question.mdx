## Customer problem

> Hi Mintlify team,
> I set up a Mintlify organization a while back and added docs.yosemite.com as our custom domain. I've since lost access to that account (I can't remember the password, and the email I registered with isn't active anymore). I've created a new Mintlify org under my current email, but when I try to add docs.yosemite.com I get: "this domain is already used by another organization." We're Yosemite, we own yosemite.com and manage the DNS through Cloudflare. Can you release the domain from my old org so I can add it to my new one?

## Context

- We verified that docs.yosemite.com is registered to an old Mintlify org the customer themselves created but can no longer access.
- The customer owns yosemite.com and controls its DNS via Cloudflare — this is verifiable.
- We're willing to release the domain, but need to confirm the customer is who they say they are (trust but verify).

## Questions

1. What is the correct path to verify domain ownership before taking any action to release the domain on our end?
2. Draft a reply to the customer.
