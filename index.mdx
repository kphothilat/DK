## Overview

This site collects real support ticket scenarios for practice. Each ticket has two pages:

- **Question** — the original customer message and any relevant context
- **Answer** — a template page to draft the reply

Use the sidebar to navigate between tickets. The final two entries (Scenario 1 and Scenario 2) are the harder, open-ended interview-style scenarios.
